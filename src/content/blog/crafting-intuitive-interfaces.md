## The Invisible UI

A great user interface is invisible. It allows the user to accomplish their goals without friction or confusion. In this article, I share my process for designing intuitive interfaces that delight users and drive engagement. From wireframing to prototyping, every step is crucial in creating a seamless experience.

### Principles of Good Design

* **Clarity:** The user should never have to guess what an element does.
* **Consistency:** Don't reinvent the wheel. Use familiar patterns.
* **Feedback:** Every action should have a clear and immediate reaction.

When you design with empathy, you anticipate the user's needs. You remove obstacles before they become frustrations. The result is a product that feels natural and effortless to use.
